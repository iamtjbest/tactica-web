Questions

Frequently asked.

Is Tactica really free?
Yes — Tactica is completely free to use. Every module, every formation prediction, every AI chat conversation, and the full FPL Scout database are available at no cost.
How does the AI know which formation is best?
Tactica fetches each team's last 5 completed matches, extracts the actual formations played, and calculates live Attack and Defence ratings from real goals scored and conceded. These ratings are fed into a trained Random Forest machine learning model that scores all 17 formations and returns the one with the highest win probability.
Which teams and competitions are covered?
Over 130 clubs across the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A, Ligue 1, Eredivisie, Primeira Liga, and more. Live match data covers every major competition including the Champions League, Europa League, and domestic cups.
Do I need to create an account?
An account lets you save your favourite team, track past analyses, and pick up your AI chat conversations across sessions. You can explore most features without signing up, but an account unlocks the full personalised experience.
Does the AI chat actually understand my squad?
Yes. The AI assistant manager is given your full squad roster, current live match data when synced, and the entire conversation history. It responds with real player names, real positions, and tactical advice grounded in your specific situation — not generic responses.
Is the web app the same as the mobile app?
Yes — the web app at app.tactica.com.ng has every feature available in the iOS and Android apps. Use whichever is most convenient; your account syncs across both.
