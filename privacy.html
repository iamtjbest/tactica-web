Privacy Policy.

How Tactica collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website, web app, and mobile app.

1. Overview

Tactica ("we", "our", "us") is a football tactical intelligence engine operated by Linea Football. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, web application, and mobile app, and how that information is used and protected.

By using Tactica, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information necessary to provide the Tactica engine and improve it over time.

CategoryExamples
Account InformationEmail address, display name, favourite team — if you create an account
Usage DataFormations analysed, teams searched, chat messages sent to the AI assistant
Device InformationDevice type, operating system, app version (for crash reporting and compatibility)
Approximate LocationCountry-level, used only to default time zone and language — never tracked precisely

We do not collect payment information, as Tactica is currently free to use.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide formation predictions, Starting XI recommendations, and tactical analysis
  • To personalise your experience — saving your favourite team and past analyses
  • To power the AI tactical chat with relevant squad and match context
  • To improve our machine learning model and overall product quality
  • To communicate important updates about the service, if you opt in
  • To detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity

4. Third-Party Services

Tactica relies on the following third-party services to function. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data passed to it:

  • BSD (Bzzoiro Sports Data) — provides live football data (scores, squads, fixtures). No personal user data is shared with this service.
  • Google Gemini — powers the AI tactical chat. Messages you send to the chat are processed by Gemini to generate responses.
  • Firebase — provides account authentication and data storage for registered users.
  • Render / Vercel — host our backend and web application infrastructure.

We do not sell your personal information to any third party, and we never will.

5. Data Storage & Security

Account data is stored securely using Firebase's infrastructure, which includes encryption in transit and at rest. Match and squad data fetched for analysis is cached temporarily to improve performance and is not linked to your personal identity.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@lineafootball.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Tactica is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the app or via email where applicable. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, reach out to us at hello@lineafootball.com or visit our Contact page.
